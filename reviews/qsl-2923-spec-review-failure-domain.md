## Summary

Ticket: QSL-2923. Checked unstated failure modes and boundary cases of the new emission route and the always/until subsystem check. Two gaps: the emission scope is wider than the entry's admission, and the failed-strict-comparison outcome is not normative.

## Findings

| ID | Severity | Summary | Refs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FND-001 | medium | The Emission bullet applies to any unwindowed eventually/always/until under INFINITE-TRACE and obliges EN-5 to emit the subsystem certificate, but the checked-subject subsystem entry admits only an empty fairness set, no timed choices, a resolved workload with no free choices and a deterministic memoryless scheduler. For a cyclic claim with fairness, timed choices, free workload choices or a randomized witness, EN-5 must emit evidence no entry can replay. Restrict the bullet to subjects the entry admits and state that others keep their existing settlement (the 'because of the cycle' clause alone does not say so). | spec/functional/FR-202-replay-a-probabilistic-witness.md:119-131 |
| FND-002 | medium | The paragraph says only 'Equality SHALL NOT refute'; it does not say how a valid certificate that fails the strict comparison is reported. AC-36/AC-37 assert `ThresholdMissed` at the initial key and `inconclusive`, while FR-201's ThresholdMissed is defined for the claim (proof) side and the path-set entry reports insufficient mass as replay parity. Two implementers could return ReplayParity or CertificateRejected{ThresholdMissed}. State it normatively, as the mean-certificate bullet does: failing the strict comparison, including equality, rejects `ThresholdMissed` at the initial product key and settles `inconclusive`. | spec/functional/FR-202-replay-a-probabilistic-witness.md:250-273 |

## Verdict

Covered well: decided-state absorption including decided states with outgoing cycles (AC-39 n/b), B precedence over A, genuine terminal stutter, producer-supplied component sets rejected, scheduler missing/randomized/wrong (AC-38), equality on both sides, an AC-37 certificate that values an accepting component (RankBroken). No hash, digest or pin use.
