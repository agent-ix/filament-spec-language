## Verdict

Not mergeable as is. Three high findings, all in FR-450 and its test case:

- FR-450-AC-12(a) uses a capture type whose value range is [0, 1], so its capture is never out of range.
- The kind rule does not cover an open-below decisive set.
- The two-period count is too low for a bounded past operator with b > P and for nested past operators.

Everything else is medium or low. The intake, align-skip, Bytes/JsonObject and flow-order edits are sound. The event-position form agrees with the single-trace closed-trace evaluator, but it needs a fairness sentence, a match rule past a component's end, and an AC that can fail.
