## Summary

Ticket: QSL-2923. Checked the new always/until event-avoidance certificate definitions, AC-36..AC-39 numbers, FR-201 notes, the AC-21 supersession and consistency with FSpec FR-411/412/413 (filament-specification origin/main 138476f9). The certificate mathematics is sound and every hand-computed value matches; two ACs assert a proof-side outcome that FR-201 does not admit, the eventual `<= θ` side is mandated but undefined, and the FSpec follow-up is untracked.

## Verdict

Verified by hand: union-of-bottom-components classification is exhaustive and exclusive after decided-state absorption, so Pr(event) = Pr(reach accepting union) = 1 − Pr(reach rejecting union); `>= θ` needs lower > 1 − θ and `<= θ` needs lower > θ; equality never refutes; checker re-derives the union. AC-36 (value 0; x(ok)=x(down)=1, ranks 1/0; 1 > 1/10; 1/10 at equality -> ThresholdMissed), AC-37 (value 1/2; 1/2 > 2/5 both sides; equality at 1/2; u loop -> RankBroken), AC-38 (min 0 via leave, max 1 via stay; stay with AC-36 certificate yields no token) and AC-39 (value 0; value 1/2 = (1/4)/(1/2); both unions 1/2 > 2/5) are correct. The AC-21 supersession changes only the cyclic sentence; nothing else is weakened. FR-201 edits are pointers, not duplicated normative text, and state no proof widening. Mergeable once the high and medium findings are fixed; the FSpec follow-up may be deferred with a filed ticket id.
