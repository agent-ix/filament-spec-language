## Verdict

Two high, four medium and two low. The hint texts name flags and members that exist: `--model <identity>=<file>` and `--library <identity>=<file>` (fsl-command/src/command.rs:328-329, check only), the compile request's `models`/`libraries` members, `byte_provision` (fsl-replay/src/request.rs:312) and `/replay/function_owner` (fsl-replay/src/execute.rs:266-272). A compile given both `--output` and an `output` member is already refused `OutputGivenTwice` (command.rs:2769-2772), so the success text never sees both. Not mergeable as is. Mergeable once FND-001, FND-002 and the medium findings are fixed.
