## Verdict

Three high findings and one medium. FR-621 and FR-624 text forms print no stage label (FR-621-AC-6 `0 Bounded refuted`, FR-624 `fsl analyze: invalid-request:` lines), so leaving them as they are does not break 'text mode names the stage in words'. The monitor and run texts that render through `render_outcome` (render.rs:2646, fsl-command/tests/it/run_text_witness.rs:122) are covered by the new header rule. The stage table is complete: its 12 labels equal `OutcomeStage` (fsl-replay/src/outcome.rs:141-166) and the `last_stage` enum of fsl-replay/schemas/filament-outcome-1.schema.json:10. Not mergeable as is. FND-001 and FND-002 also need an architecture decision.
