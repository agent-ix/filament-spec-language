## Summary

Ticket: QSL-2306. The diff answers the four questions from PR #2748. It was checked against FR-327, FR-328, FR-330 (AC-1, AC-2, AC-12), FR-501 (basis rule, "Established window", AC-10, AC-14), FR-502 (closure monotonicity), FR-504 ("Open scope", Inputs), FR-641 (trace kinds and `G`) and FR-642 (Inputs, Outputs, AC-2).

Ids: FR-820 and TC-2320..TC-2323 are new, in range (FR-820..829, TC-2320..2339), and each appears once. `PATH= quire validate --scope . 'spec/**/*.md'` exits 0, with no warning on any changed file.

Rulings 1 (deciding-prefix report plus category and truth agreement) and 2 (`end` closes the stream) hold together for the `Finite`, `Progressed` and `Timestamped` kinds. Ruling 4 fits FR-194 and FR-330. Ruling 3 has three defects. The infinite-trace bullet contradicts FR-328. The finite-window bullet's second disjunct is unsound under `on each`. And the headline "at the first call ... and at no other call" is a semantic condition that the per-profile evaluators only approximate. The Timed kind is left with no closure. FR-820 lacks the second reading and the progress-only step that FR-643 needs.

## Verdict

REQUEST CHANGES, for FND-001 to FND-008.

Nothing else is narrowed. FR-643-AC-5's switch from an infinite-trace clause to `Recent` follows from ruling 4: an infinite-trace clause retains no values. The `Progressed` closure moves from `end` to the progress assertion, which FR-502 already governs. The one capability lost is the Timed kind's closure (FND-004).

Ruling 1 is coherent with FR-502 and FR-641. The deciding-prefix report never changes. Category and truth agreement over longer prefixes follows from soundness. FR-644-AC-5's split between `category` (over 0..k) and `disposition` (over 0..j) states the rule exactly for `Never`.

Ruling 2 is coherent for `Finite` (FR-327, FR-328, FR-641's `finite` row) and for `Progressed` and `Timestamped` (FR-502 makes closure monotone, so a later record cannot reopen a scope).

Ruling 4: FR-820's summarized-positions rule matches FR-194 window aggregates and bounded-operator horizons. Its Equality and Bytes contracts can be tested once FND-005 to FND-007 are fixed.
